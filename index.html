Tareeq: Driving Theory Test: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Tareeq is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any government authority or licensing body.

1. Who we are

Tareeq is an Android app (package com.bahar.tareeq) published by Bahar Uddin Raihan. Contact: dev.raihan.tech@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or files.

The app stores the following only on your device (using Android local storage): your chosen language, how many questions you answered and got right, your best mock-test score, the questions you mastered or got wrong, your study streak, and whether you accepted the disclaimer. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use certain data from your device to show ads and measure their performance, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. The data AdMob collects is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the app menu (Privacy options).

4. How we use information

We do not use your information ourselves, other than the on-device progress data described above to show your progress. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing

We do not share information with anyone, except that AdMob (Google) receives data as described in section 3 when ads are shown.

6. Children

The app is intended for people old enough to take a driving test and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security and retention

Progress data stays on your device until you delete it. We do not hold your data on any server.

8. Your choices

You can delete all app data by clearing storage or uninstalling the app, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, and manage ad consent from the app menu.

9. Accuracy of content

Questions are original practice material and may differ from the official test. Traffic laws change; always check official sources.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

11. Contact

dev.raihan.tech@gmail.com
